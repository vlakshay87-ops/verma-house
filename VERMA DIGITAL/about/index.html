About Verma Digital

Digital growth starts with understanding the business.

Verma Digital brings strategy, advertising, search visibility and conversion-focused web experiences together around the people a business wants to reach.

A founder-led point of view

Meet Lakshay Verma.

Founded by Lakshay Verma, Verma Digital is built around a simple idea: digital marketing should begin with understanding the business, its customers, and the reason someone would choose one solution over another.

We focus on combining marketing strategy, performance advertising, search visibility, and conversion-focused web experiences to help businesses build a stronger digital presence. The work begins with customer intent and market context, then turns those observations into clear messaging, useful experiences and measurable next steps.

“Good marketing should make the next decision clearer—for the business and for the person it hopes to serve.”

A working principle at Verma Digital

How we approach the work

From the business question to a better-informed next step.

Marketing is not just the channel. It is the full journey between what a business offers and how a customer understands it.

Understand

Clarify goals, customers, the offer and the constraints that shape decisions.

Research

Consider competitors, search demand, customer needs and market opportunities.

Execute

Develop the most relevant campaigns, website experience and measurement plan.

Learn

Review signals, explain findings and prioritise sensible improvements.

Our working principles

Clear thinking, honest collaboration.

We believe thoughtful marketing is grounded in the realities of a business—not a promise of guaranteed rankings or leads.

  • Strategy before execution
  • Business objectives before vanity metrics
  • Transparent communication and reporting
  • Data-informed decisions, with proper context
  • Conversion-focused implementation
  • Continuous improvement without overclaiming certainty

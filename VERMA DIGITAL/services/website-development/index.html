Frequently asked questions

Will the site work on mobile?

Responsive behaviour is planned for mobile, tablet and desktop layouts and tested across common screen sizes. Device and browser combinations still vary, so continued testing is useful.

Can my site collect enquiries?

Yes, using a properly configured form and secure lead backend. Backend credentials, deployment and email delivery must be configured before real submissions can be stored.

Will a new website guarantee more leads?

No. A website can improve clarity and usability, but traffic quality, offer fit, market conditions and follow-up also affect enquiries.

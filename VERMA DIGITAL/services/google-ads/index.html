  • Search term and keyword relevance

A click is not the same as a qualified lead. Measurement should distinguish meaningful enquiries from page views and other low-intent activity.

Frequently asked questions

What budget should I start with?

There is no universal starting figure. Budget depends on target geography, search competition, expected click costs, conversion measurement and how much you can invest in learning responsibly.

Will Google Ads guarantee top placement?

No. Ad positions and delivery depend on auction conditions, relevance, bid strategy and platform policies. Placement cannot be guaranteed.

Do you include landing page recommendations?

Yes. The landing experience influences relevance and action, so campaign review should include the page people reach after a click.

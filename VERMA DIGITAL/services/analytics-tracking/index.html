Good decisions need trustworthy signals.

Missing or duplicated tags, poorly named events and unclear conversion definitions can make dashboards confusing. Teams may optimise toward clicks or page views while real enquiries remain hard to assess.

Tracking should start with the actions that matter to your business, how they can be measured responsibly and who needs to use the information. Platform attribution is imperfect; reporting should communicate limitations as well as numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Do analytics tell me exactly which channel caused a sale?

Not always. Attribution models rely on available identifiers and platform methods, and they can differ. Use them as directional context alongside your own lead and sales records.

Do I need Google Tag Manager?

Not necessarily. It can help manage tags, but the appropriate approach depends on the website, existing setup, governance and tracking needs.

Can you track callback form submissions?

Yes, once the form and tracking setup are configured. Events should represent successful submissions, not just button clicks, and should avoid sending sensitive personal information to analytics.

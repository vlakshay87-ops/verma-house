Privacy Policy

This starter policy explains how Verma Digital intends to handle information submitted through this website. Confirm the business-specific details before launch.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Information you submit

When you use an enquiry or callback form, the website requests your name, Indian mobile number, optional business name, optional service interest, optional preferred contact time, consent and the page where the enquiry was sent. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a form.

How we use it

Verma Digital uses submitted information to respond to your enquiry, understand the service you are asking about and manage follow-up. If email notifications are configured, relevant enquiry details may also be sent to a business-configured notification address. No automatic WhatsApp notification is sent when you submit a form.

Storage and access

When the secure backend is configured, form submissions are stored in a private Supabase database. Public website visitors cannot read stored leads. Authorised project administrators can access and manage them through the Supabase dashboard. The website owner must configure access, retention and deletion practices appropriate to the business.

Until the lead backend is configured, the form will not claim to submit successfully and will direct you to call or message instead. Email notification is an optional additional delivery channel; persistent database storage is the record of an enquiry.

Service providers and international processing

Supabase may host submitted data in the project region selected by the website owner. If configured, Resend may process notification emails. The rate limiter retains a short-lived HMAC fingerprint derived from the request IP to enforce a limit; the raw IP is not written to the lead record. These providers handle information under their own terms and policies. Confirm provider locations, account settings and any applicable transfer requirements before launch.

Cookies, analytics and third-party links

This starter website does not intentionally set analytics cookies or include an analytics tag by default. If analytics, advertising pixels, embedded content or other tracking tools are added, update this policy and configure appropriate notice and consent controls before use. Links to WhatsApp and telephone services are provided for convenience and are governed by those providers’ own terms.

Retention and your requests

Enquiry information should be retained only as long as needed for follow-up and legitimate business record-keeping. The owner must set and follow a retention schedule. Expired rate-limit fingerprints are deleted when the endpoint next processes a valid submission. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted, contact Verma Digital by phone at +91 93196 72630 or use the WhatsApp link on the contact page.

Security

Database access is restricted by row-level security and submissions are handled by a server-side function. No online transmission or storage method is risk-free. Keep administrative credentials secure, limit dashboard access and do not put service-role keys in website code.

Business details to confirm

Before publishing, the owner should confirm the data controller/contact details, applicable retention period, provider locations, any analytics or tracking services added, how data requests are handled, and whether a separate business email or address should be listed.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. Questions can be directed to Verma Digital at +91 93196 72630. This starter policy is not legal advice; the business owner should review it for their circumstances.
